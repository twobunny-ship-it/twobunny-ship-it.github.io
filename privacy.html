RhetorIQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

RhetorIQ ("the app") is made by Two Bunny Labs ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We built RhetorIQ to work without accounts and to keep your data on your device wherever possible.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact information through the app. Your learning progress — lessons completed, streaks, hearts, and mastery — is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us.

Third-party services

RhetorIQ uses a small number of third-party services to show optional ads and to process purchases. These services may receive limited technical information (such as a device advertising identifier, app version, and general device details) as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — serves the optional "watch an ad to refill hearts" rewarded videos. We request non-personalized ads only, and we do not ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and whether you have unlocked Premium. It receives an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase/transaction details, not your name. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — processes the actual payment for any purchase through the App Store. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Ads in RhetorIQ are optional — they appear only if you choose to watch one to refill hearts. We request non-personalized ads, meaning they are based on general context rather than your personal profile, and we do not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt because we do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

RhetorIQ Premium is a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see or store your payment card or billing details. RevenueCat records that a purchase was made so the app can unlock Premium features and restore them if you reinstall.

Children's privacy

RhetorIQ is intended for high-school students and general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for teen audiences.

Your choices

You can clear all locally stored progress by deleting the app. You can limit ad personalization system-wide in your device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
